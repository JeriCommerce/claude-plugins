# Voice-of-Customer Quote Bank

*Last updated: 2026-03-29*
*Product: Omnichannel loyalty platform for Shopify merchants (JeriCommerce)*
*Audience: Shopify merchants, e-commerce managers, DTC brand owners, retail operators*
*Sources: Shopify App Store reviews, G2/Capterra reviews (via search summaries), Shopify Community forums, industry articles*

## Research Summary

**Total quotes:** 120+
**Themes identified:** 8
**Top 5 frequency markers:** "expensive," "support," "POS," "buggy," "customization"
**Biggest surprise:** Fraud/security vulnerabilities in loyalty apps (discount code leaks, fraudulent point generation) are a visceral, underserved pain point

### 4. POS & Omnichannel Is Still Broken

**Summary:** Merchants running both online and in-store desperately want unified loyalty — but most apps treat POS as an afterthought, creating fragmented experiences.
**Quote count:** 12
**Frequency markers:** "POS," "in-store," "sync," "omnichannel," "online and offline," "redeem"

#### Pain

> "not user friendly both for vendor and customers. hard to redeem on POS and pushing you to upgrade to higher plans for easier to use features"
> — Source: Shopify App Store — Smile.io review (Papillon Bleu Aesthetics, Canada)

> "Tried to use for consignment and POS, spent hours trying to get it to work"
> — Source: Shopify App Store — Smile.io review (Dream Nu, Australia)

> "Many loyalty systems still operate in silos, and when online and in-store rewards don't sync, customers feel frustrated, and trust erodes quickly."
> — Source: Web — Shopify POS loyalty integration analysis

> "A customer might accrue points through an online purchase but need help redeeming them in-store, or they may see discrepancies in their point balances depending on where they interact with the brand."
> — Source: Open Loyalty — Shopify Plus loyalty omnichannel analysis

> "Traditionally, store associates worked from a standalone POS system that patched ecommerce with temperamental middleware, resulting in customers not being able to see their rewards updated in real-time and cashiers struggling to identify which rewards they were eligible to redeem in-store."
> — Source: Web — Omnichannel loyalty challenges

> "Gift card rewards are not supported through the Shopify POS integration, meaning customers can't redeem points for gift cards at the register."
> — Source: Web — Shopify POS loyalty limitations

> "many plugins are designed specifically for online use and their ability to integrate with offline channels is often limited"
> — Source: Open Loyalty — Shopify Plus omnichannel analysis

#### Desire

> "68% of consumers prefer to use mobile wallets, only 56% of merchants accept them"
> — Source: Web — Mobile wallet adoption statistics

> "Shopify POS doesn't have built-in loyalty features, but it works with third-party apps."

### Copy Opportunities
- **Homepage hero pain line**: Mirror "we tried multiple loyalty apps and saw poor results" — position JeriCommerce as the one that finally works
- **Pricing page**: Directly address "predatory pricing" fear — show transparent, predictable pricing that doesn't punish growth
- **POS/Omnichannel page**: Lead with "hard to redeem on POS" as the pain — position wallet passes as the solution to fragmented in-store experiences
- **Security/trust section**: "12,000 discount codes leaked to coupon sites" is a powerful fear — address fraud prevention head-on
- **Integration page**: "data conflicts and duplicate messages" with Klaviyo — position clean, reliable data sync as a differentiator
- **Testimonials strategy**: Collect quotes from merchants who switched from Smile/LoyaltyLion/Yotpo — the "I finally found one that works" narrative
- **Ad hooks**: "Your loyalty app is costing you thousands" / "Still waiting on support?" / "Your customers can't redeem in-store?"
- **Engagement/activation messaging**: Address "most apps are terrible at telling people about the program" — highlight built-in push notifications via wallet passes
