# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-03-30*

## Product

**Name:** JeriCommerce
**Category:** Omnichannel loyalty platform for Shopify
**One-liner:** The loyalty platform built for how Shopify merchants actually sell.
**Site:** https://jericommerce.com

### What it does
Loyalty programs (points, rewards, VIP tiers, referrals) delivered through native wallet passes (Apple Wallet, Google Wallet) with push notifications, NFC/QR identification, and deep Shopify POS integration. Also integrates with Klaviyo and Shopify Flow.

### What makes it different
- **Wallet-first:** Loyalty cards live in the customer's existing wallet app. No app download, no friction. 90% push notification open rates vs. 2% email.
- **Actually omnichannel:** Online and in-store rewards sync through one profile. POS identification via NFC tap or QR scan, not manual email lookups.
- **No feature gating:** Core features available on every plan. Merchants don't pay more just to unlock what they need.
- **Shopify-native:** Built on Shopify's infrastructure (POS tiles, Flow triggers, checkout extension, Multipass). No middleware, no data conflicts.

### Proof points
- 5.0 stars on Shopify App Store (12 reviews)
- 200+ Shopify brands
- 90% push notification open rate
- 15s customer enrollment time
- 3x higher repeat purchase rate
- Named clients: Fairfax & Favor, Nude Project, Blue Banana Brand, Siwon, WOW Concept, Aureline, Old Jeffrey

## Audience

### Primary ICP
**Shopify merchants with physical retail** who have tried loyalty apps before and been burned.

Typical profiles:
- E-commerce managers at DTC brands doing 6-7 figures
- Retail operators managing both online + physical stores
- Marketing directors at Shopify Plus brands

### What they've experienced (from VoC research)
They've used Smile.io, LoyaltyLion, Yotpo, Growave, BON, or Rivo and hit the same walls:
- Pricing that punishes growth (features locked behind $400+/month plans)
- Support that disappears when something breaks
- Buggy apps that cost real money (leaked discount codes, phantom rewards)
- POS that doesn't actually sync with online
- Integration nightmares with Klaviyo (data conflicts, duplicate messages)
- Programs nobody uses because the app makes it too hard

### How they talk about their problems
Use their language, not ours:

| They say | We used to say (avoid) |
|----------|----------------------|
| "hard to redeem on POS" | "Omnichannel loyalty" |
| "pushing you to upgrade" | "Free plan available" |
| "nobody uses the program" | "Boost retention" |
| "discount codes leaked to coupon sites" | "Secure rewards" |
| "support disappeared" | "Dedicated support" |
| "buggy mess out of the box" | "Easy setup" |
| "can't sync online and in-store" | "Unified experience" |
| "Klaviyo data breaks" | "Klaviyo integration" |

### What they want to hear
- That this one actually works
- That pricing is transparent and won't spike
- That POS sync is real, not a marketing claim
- That someone will answer when something breaks
- That setup is fast and doesn't require a developer
