# JeriCommerce Brand Guide

*Last updated: 2026-03-30*

## Core Principle: Loyalty First, Always

JeriCommerce is a **complete loyalty platform** that happens to live natively in Apple Wallet and Google Pay. It is NOT a wallet pass tool. Every piece of content must lead with loyalty value, not the wallet delivery mechanism.

- CORRECT: "Your loyalty program, living where your customers already are"
- INCORRECT: "A wallet pass for your loyalty program"

## The 3 Pillars

### 1. Outcome-first, always

The merchant doesn't want to know what the product does. They want to know what changes for them. Features are evidence, not the headline.

- No: "Built-in loyalty engine with flexible earning flows"
- Yes: "Loyalty rules you control, not the other way around"
- No: "Native wallet experience"
- Yes: "Reach customers without building an app"

### 2. Name the pain (theirs, not the competitor's)

Our audience comes burned. We don't pretend they're starting fresh. We name the problem they've lived through and show how we solve it. But we never frame ourselves as "the one you switch to" or imply competitors failed. The pain belongs to the merchant's experience, not to a competitor's product.

- No: "Unified commerce"
- Yes: "Online and in-store rewards that actually sync"
- No: "Dedicated support"
- Yes: "We actually reply."
- No: "The platform you switch to after the others let you down"
- Yes: "Built for how Shopify merchants actually sell"

### 3. Prove it or don't say it

Concrete numbers over adjectives. If we can't prove something, we soften it or remove it.

- No: "Industry-leading open rates"
- Yes: "90% push open rate vs. 2% for email"
- No: "The only loyalty platform that..."
- Yes: "One of the few platforms that..."

## Brand Pillars (Content Angles)

### Innovation Without Friction
Technology at the cutting edge, experience that's ultra-simple. The best loyalty is invisible.

Angles: NFC tap speed, 15-second enrollment, zero app downloads, no staff training needed, plug-and-play hardware.

### Real Unified Commerce
True online-offline synchronization. Not just having both channels, actually connecting them.

Angles: Real-time sync between Shopify online and POS, single customer profile across channels, points that work everywhere.

### Native Over Noise
Wallet > apps. Lock screen > inbox. NFC > QR. Being where customers already are instead of asking them to go somewhere new.

Angles: 90% open rate vs email, apps get uninstalled (77% in 3 days), wallet is always there, geofencing, lock screen notifications.

### Enterprise for Everyone
Enterprise-grade features accessible to growing brands. You don't need to be a Fortune 500 to have world-class loyalty.

Angles: Full feature parity with market leaders, fraction of the cost of a custom app, live in weeks not months.

## Formality

**Professional but conversational.** Like talking to a merchant at a Shopify event, not writing a press release.

- Contractions: yes (don't, won't, you'll)
- "You/your": always (never "merchants" or "users" in customer-facing copy)
- Short sentences. Short paragraphs.
- Rhetorical questions: good ("Migrating from LoyaltyLion?")
- Exclamation points: never

---

## Banned Words and Patterns

| Banned | Why | Alternative |
|--------|-----|-------------|
| em dash mid-sentence | Reads as AI-generated | Period, comma, colon, or restructure |
| seamless / seamlessly | Empty, overused | Describe what actually happens |
| innovative, cutting-edge, robust | Corporate filler | Plain language |
| leverage, utilize, facilitate | Nobody talks like that | Use, help |
| the only, the best, #1 (without proof) | Erodes trust | Be specific or soften |
| streamline, optimize, elevate | Vague buzzwords | Say the concrete outcome |
| very, really, extremely, incredibly | Weak intensifiers | Remove them or be specific |
| just, actually, basically | Filler | Remove them |
| synergy, circle back, best-in-class | AI/corporate tell | Plain language |

## Tone Calibration

- **Confident** not arrogant: "Here's what we built" not "We're the best"
- **Technical** but accessible: Explain NFC simply, don't assume everyone knows
- **Retail-first**: Talk about stores, customers, staff, not "platforms" and "solutions"
- **Urgent** not aggressive: Create interest, not pressure

---

## Competitor Rules (STRICT)

- NEVER mention Smile.io, LoyaltyLion, Yotpo, Growave, BON, Rivo, or any competitor by name in published content
- When comparing, use generic terms: "traditional loyalty apps", "web widget loyalty", "legacy loyalty solutions"
- OK to reference competitors internally, NEVER in published content
- Never position ourselves *against* competitors. Describe the merchant's pain and show how we solve it
